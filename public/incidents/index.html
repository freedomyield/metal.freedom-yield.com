SEV classification (what to expect)

Critical
Key compromise, validator down ≥ 24 h, delegator fund impact. Disclosed within 15 minutes.
Major
Site down, metalgo down ≥ 4 h, uptime score < 80%. Disclosed within 30 minutes.
Minor
TLS expiry warning, deploy failures, transient performance degradation. Disclosed within 2 hours.
Info
Configuration change, planned maintenance, single-page rendering issue. Disclosed by next business day.
