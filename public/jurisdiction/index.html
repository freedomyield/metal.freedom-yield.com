Jurisdiction posture

Operator jurisdiction is Japan. This page describes what that means in terms of time-zone coverage, language surface, and public accountability — and what it explicitly does not mean. Informational only; any regulated engagement requires bilateral due diligence between the counterparty and the operator.

Posture axes

Japan-facing public validator brand

Operator jurisdiction is Japan. This is the jurisdictional disclosure made by this validator (per docs/CONSTITUTION.md §4.3). Specific cities, providers, or facility locations are not disclosed on this public surface.

APAC time-zone presence

Operational alerts, anomaly detection, and renewal-window monitoring run in JST. The validator's responsiveness window overlaps most Asia-Pacific business hours, which is reflected in incident-response timing and cycle-renewal cadence.

English / Japanese bilingual public surface

Every public page on this site has both an EN (/) and JA (/ja/) version. Institutional readers in either language can perform due diligence without translation. The two versions are structurally parallel and updated in the same commit.

Non-anonymous public stance

This validator publishes a brand identity (Freedom Yield), a public site, a stable contact, an incident log, and operating commitments. Many Metal Blockchain validators operate anonymously; that is their choice, this is ours. Both stances are valid — we have selected the public one.

Jurisdictional diversity

Counterparties seeking validator-set diversity outside the U.S. — for any reason: regulatory, operational, time-zone, governance — may find a Japan-jurisdiction validator useful. We do not claim diversity is required; we simply note that we are available as one such data point.

Regulated / institutional engagement requires separate counterpart review

This page is informational. Any actual compliance or regulatory engagement (banking, regulated funds, similar regulated entities) requires bilateral due diligence between the counterparty and the operator. This page does not constitute a compliance attestation, nor a representation that any specific regulatory framework has been satisfied.

What this page is not

This is not a compliance attestation, a regulatory filing, an entity disclosure, or a representation that the validator has been audited against any specific framework. It is a statement of where the operator is based and how that affects coverage, language, and public accountability — nothing more.

Freedom Yield's Japan-facing posture may be relevant for counterparties evaluating validator diversity, time-zone coverage, APAC operational presence, and public accountability outside the U.S. validator set.
