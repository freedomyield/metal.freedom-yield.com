About this record

This page is the long-horizon ledger. Each entry corresponds to one closed AddPermissionlessValidatorTx cycle on the Metal Blockchain P-Chain. Once written, entries are not edited — if a mistake is discovered, a correction is appended as the next cycle's note, and the original stays as the immutable record.

Operator notes are intentionally short — what happened, what was learned, what changed for next cycle. They are not a marketing channel; they are the part of the audit trail a tx ID alone can't carry.
