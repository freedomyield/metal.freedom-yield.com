Transparent Validator Pledge

A minimum disclosure standard for blockchain validator operators, released into the public domain under CC0. Five articles covering uptime, incidents, fee stability, decommission notice, and identity. Any validator on any chain can adopt it, modify it, or fork it. Freedom Yield publishes it as a specimen and a proposing party — not as an authority.

What it is

A short, public-domain text describing the minimum artifacts a validator can publish to make itself evaluable by delegators and reviewers without ad-hoc back-and-forth. It is a baseline, not a ceiling.

Why

Every reviewer of every validator ends up asking the same handful of questions — uptime history, incident handling, fee stability, exit notice, who is on the other side. A shared minimum-disclosure pattern reduces friction for reviewers and creates fewer surprises during selection. Anonymous operation remains a valid choice; the Pledge is for validators who have chosen a public stance.

The five articles

  • Article 1 — Uptime track record. A machine-readable record of uptime by cycle, cross-checkable on chain.
  • Article 2 — Incident transparency. A log of operational incidents with date, severity, impact, root cause, and resolution.
  • Article 3 — Fee stability. Current fee published; changes announced in advance with a defined notice period.
  • Article 4 — Decommission notice. A pre-committed minimum lead time for any decision to stop operating.
  • Article 5 — Identity disclosure standard. Brand identity, stable contact, and jurisdiction at country granularity — no more is required.

Full text: docs/TRANSPARENT_VALIDATOR_PLEDGE.md ↗

Freedom Yield as specimen

Freedom Yield adopts the Pledge and publishes the following mapping. Other adopters may map differently — there is no required URL layout.

  • Article 1 — Uptime track record: /journal/ + /api/uptime-cycles.json
  • Article 2 — Incident transparency: /incidents/ + /api/incidents.json
  • Article 3 — Fee stability: /commitments/ (§ Fee policy) + /api/validator.json
  • Article 4 — Decommission notice: /commitments/ (§ Decommission notice) — 90 days
  • Article 5 — Identity disclosure: /jurisdiction/ + brand "Freedom Yield" + info@metal.freedom-yield.com

License

The Pledge is released into the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal. No attribution required. Forks, derivatives, and translations are all permitted. There is no central registry; adoption is signaled at the adopter's own URL.

Adopting it

  • Publish a compliance statement on a stable URL on your own site, declaring which version of the Pledge you adhere to.
  • Optionally publish a machine-readable adoption manifest (suggested filename /api/pledge-adoption.json) following the schema sketch in the standard text.
  • Link your compliance URL from your repository's README so reviewers can find it.

Contributing

Improvements, translations, and forks are welcome via pull requests. Maintainer authority is purely editorial; any disagreement that cannot be resolved editorially may be resolved by forking.
