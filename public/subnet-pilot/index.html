Subnet pilot

This page exists so a subnet operator evaluating validators can find an explicit statement that this validator is open to a bilateral pilot conversation. It is intentionally passive: there is no outbound pitch, no pre-positioning toward any specific subnet, and no bid attached. Read it as a one-line confirmation, not a sales document.

  1. Home
  2. Subnet pilot

The statement

Freedom Yield is open to bilateral conversation with subnet operators evaluating candidate validators. Engagement is expected to be quiet and reciprocal: the subnet operator initiates, the operator here responds, both sides exchange enough material to decide whether the fit is worth pursuing further. No public announcement of any kind is implied.

What this page is not

  • Not a bid for any specific subnet. The operator does not have a target subnet in mind and is not optimising the validator's posture toward one.
  • Not a solicitation. The operator does not pursue retail outreach (commitment #7) and does not self-introduce to Metallicus or to subnet operators uninvited. This page is the entirety of the operator's outbound on this topic.
  • Not a marketing surface. If a subnet operator never finds this page, the operator here is fine with that. The signal is for someone already evaluating.
  • Not pre-positioning. Capabilities and limitations are disclosed honestly on the scorecard — the same scorecard a subnet operator would see whether or not bilateral conversation ever happens.

What we would bring

The substantive readiness profile is the eight-axis scorecard. The headline picks for a subnet operator scanning quickly:

Active primary-network validator
Continuous mainnet cycles since 2026-05-19, with the audit trail at /journal/ + /api/uptime-cycles.json.
Topology already segregated
Validator workload and public web are on separate hosts; the topology and key-separation principles are documented at /reference-architecture/.
APAC time-zone coverage
Operator jurisdiction is Japan; APAC business-hours alert response. Bilingual EN/JA surface.
Transparent operating posture
9 unilateral commitments, 7 honest risk disclosures, public incident log, machine-readable evidence manifest at /api/evidence.json.

What we would need

Pilot-shape questions that require operator-side answers and cannot be pre-committed at this surface:

  • Capacity sizing for the additional metalgo process (CPU, RAM, disk, bandwidth).
  • Response-time and uptime expectations specific to the subnet, including any reward/slashing parameters.
  • Subnet-specific compliance posture (KYC, regulatory scope, data residency).
  • Custody framework expectations for any subnet-specific key material.
  • Communication cadence: incident escalation path, status reporting, change-management notice.

None of the above is pre-committed publicly because pre-committing without context would be exactly the inflated-claim posture commitment #4 rules out.

How to engage

Send a single email to info@metal.freedom-yield.com. The detailed inquiry surface is /inquiry/ — first response within 24 hours on Japan business days, no required identifier, PGP available on request. A throwaway address is fine.

If the conversation should move to another channel after the first round, say so in the first message and the operator will follow.

Reference

The technical artefacts a subnet operator typically needs to decide whether to open the conversation:

  • /reference-architecture/ — two-host topology, push-only data flow, three-key separation
  • /selection-evidence/ — twelve-row evidence index
