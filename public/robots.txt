# Current crawl policy: closed.
#
# This site is published for institutional readers who arrive at a specific
# URL — not for search-engine discovery. Indexing is suppressed at three
# layers (defense in depth):
#   1. <meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow"> on every HTML page
#   2. X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet on every response
#   3. robots.txt Disallow for the wildcard and for every major
#      search / AI crawler we are aware of (this file)
#
# The explicit per-bot Disallow lines exist because Cloudflare may inject an
# "Allow: /" against User-agent: * higher up in the file. Specific User-agent
# rules take precedence over the wildcard per the robots.txt specification,
# so the per-bot entries make our intent unambiguous regardless of any CDN
# managed-content injection.
#
# If the operator decides to open the site to search engines (e.g., after a
# Metallicus public announcement), the steps are: remove or flip this file,
# remove the X-Robots-Tag in caddy/Caddyfile, and update each page's
# meta robots tag. Until that decision is made, all three layers stay on.
