Why these numbers matter (and the hardware does not)

On Snowman consensus, hardware above the official minimum (8 vCPU / 16 GiB) does not increase rewards. What matters for delegators is:

  • Self-stake

    How much skin we have in the game. Intentionally small; live value in the home-page status card.

  • Uptime score

    What the rest of the network thinks of our availability. Must stay above 80% for rewards.

  • Delegation fee

    The cut we keep from delegator rewards. Protocol minimum 2%, we set 3% — predictable, no surprise hikes.

  • Incident history

    Transparency about past outages and how we handled them. See incidents.

Risk profile

No slashing

Principal safe

Avalanche family chains do not slash stakes. The worst case is "no rewards for this staking period." Your principal (the METAL you delegated) is always returned when the end time arrives, regardless of our uptime.

Lock period

Notice

Delegation locks your tokens from 14 days minimum to 1 year maximum. You cannot withdraw early. During the lock period your METAL is at price-risk in the market.

Single-operator risk

Diversify

Our validator is a single node. If the host fails and we cannot recover within the staking period, you would miss rewards for that period. Diversifying across multiple validators is good practice.

How to delegate (when live)

  1. Acquire METAL on a supported exchange or P2P.
  2. Open Metal Wallet, create or import a wallet.
  3. Move tokens to P-Chain (cross-chain transfer from X-Chain).
  4. Choose "Earn" → "Add Delegator". Paste our NodeID (shown above when live).
  5. Set the lock period (14d–1y). Confirm and send.
  6. After the start time you can verify the delegation on explorer.metalblockchain.org.

More detail in our internal runbook docs/TAHOE_PIPELINE.md (same flow on Tahoe testnet, free via faucet).
